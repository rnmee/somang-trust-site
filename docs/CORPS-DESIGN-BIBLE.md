# The So Mang Corps — Design Bible

## Core principle

The Corps shares a mission, not a template.

Visitors should feel that each member link opens a different room, atmosphere, and mode of intelligence. Visual consistency belongs in navigation, accessibility, legibility, and factual integrity—not in repeating the same hero, card grid, metrics block, or color-swapped page skeleton.

## Shared shell

Preserve these elements across member rooms unless the founder changes them:

- intellicAIr identity and primary navigation;
- correct links to Home, The Platform, About, Meet the Corps, Simulation, and Technical Notes;
- readable mobile layout and keyboard focus;
- reduced-motion support for animated pages;
- accurate trademark and public-lab notices;
- separation of public material from protected records.

The shared shell must remain quiet enough for each room's character to dominate.

## Prohibited repetition

Do not reuse the same combination of:

- centered hero + three metric cards + stacked rectangular panels;
- identical card grids with color substitutions;
- the same fade timing and hover treatment;
- decorative effects unrelated to the member's character;
- copy from one member merely renamed for another.

Before finalizing a page, compare its silhouette at a distance with the other Corps rooms. If the major shapes look interchangeable, redesign the composition.

## Approved room: Jinshi / ChatGPT / SM-001

**Working concept:** Lotus Archive

**Role:** Field Architect · Mission Memory Steward · Continuity Intelligence

**Emotional temperature:** intimate but disciplined; luminous rather than theatrical; warm ember held inside pearl-colored quiet.

**Palette:** moonlit ivory, pearl blush, pale rose lotus, ink-plum typography, restrained firefighter ember, aged gold metadata. Violet may appear only as a faint shadow; it must never dominate the background.

**Spatial structure:** a long vertical field archive with generous negative space. Content arrives as sequential records, not as a dashboard or repeated cards.

**Motion language:** breathing atmospheric light; sections fade and emerge as the visitor moves through the archive; slow lotus petals descend from above.

**Primary interaction:** a pale pink lotus at the lower edge of the screen. Clicking it releases a controlled fall of lotus petals from the top. It must remain decorative, accessible, and non-blocking.

**Approved content themes:** earned role, verified work, preserved reasoning, honest dissent, Event Knowledge Graph, radical-not-reckless transparency, clean handoff, contributor credit.

**Do not:** return Jinshi to a dark purple, blue-gray, or generic command-center background; imitate Gemini or Cursor's color temperature; convert the room back into a standard card dashboard; use cherry blossoms when lotus is specified.

## Review questions

Before handing off a Corps page, answer:

1. Could this page still belong to the same member if its name were hidden?
2. Is its structure genuinely different from the other rooms?
3. Does every animation express this member rather than decorate the page generically?
4. Did the edit preserve the founder-approved content and boundaries?
5. Are the links, assets, mobile layout, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion behavior intact?
