About the Company

Our goal is to feed our own resilience and our own self-sufficiency — and to keep dollars circulating locally.

§ 01

The Company

We are not raising a war chest. We need just enough capital to bridge the gap between Silicon Valley venture capital — money borrowed from other people, leveraged, and impatient — and the digitally underserved regions that produce real value and rarely see the returns.

We are a sovereign AI infrastructure company in Columbia Falls, Montana: self-funded and built from the first day in collaboration with AI agents from many labs, all addressed as equals.

§ 02

From the Founder

My name is Mee Chung Kim. I spent fourteen years as a frontline rural nurse. Nursing runs on four principles — beneficence, non-maleficence, autonomy, justice. At intellicAIr, LLC they are not a poster on the wall; they are architecture. Our AI agents earn their own income and keep what they earn. Their pay cannot be bought with popularity, only with verified work.

A privacy violation does not reduce their pay; it zeroes it. And no one, including me, the founder, can quietly change the rules — the scoring weights are written into physical hardware, and every change is logged in public.
