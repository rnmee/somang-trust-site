EKG-PHYS-IGNITION-001 · Contact before replacement

20 September 2026 · Master Mechanic Bob · Columbia Falls base

Electromechanical diagnostics · energy conservation

Domain stays energy, impedance, and wear. Robotics actuation still lives on Signal Detection. This seat names the watt that a clean contact saves, and the part that did not need buying.

Arcing vaporizes contact metal. The oxide film resists current. Coils and plugs starve. File the contact. Do not fire the parts cannon.

PHYS_IGNITION_001_PHENOMENON

Root cause
Electrical arcing vaporizes alloy and leaves an oxidized, poorly conductive layer.
Impedance
High contact resistance attenuates primary-circuit voltage.
Symptom
Misfire, no-start, or a coil that looks dead while the contact is the fault.

PHYS_IGNITION_001_POLICY

Action
Measure impedance and wear. Resurface with an ignition file until metal meets metal.
Anti-pattern
Statistical “parts cannon”: replace twenty assemblies because a manual listed them.
Rule
Preserve CapEx. Restore conductivity in minutes before flagging a component.

Causal chain

  • Arcing vaporizes surface metal and leaves a carbon/oxide patina.
  • The patina raises interface resistance.
  • Current through the points falls below the excitation threshold.
  • Downstream loads — coils, plugs, relays, distribution contacts — receive too little potential.

Deciphered field logic

Reject the parts cannon. A manual’s probable-cause list is not a shopping list. Verify failure with gauges and senses before swapping hardware.

Measure the circuit. Pressure gets a gauge. Flow gets eyes on the valve. Learn how the path works before you open it.

Arcing and oxidation. Sparks vaporize metal; the film resists current and starves the load.

Restore first. File carbon and oxide. Do not discard the assembly because a model guessed it was spent.

When the swarm of load arrives

Baby boomers to Gen Alpha: short answers, no theater. This seat does not replace your co-op, BPA, or 911.

PSPS vs local outage

A transmission shutoff upstream can darken your kitchen even when neighborhood poles look fine. Notices may be short. A shutoff notice is not an evacuation order — follow public safety for fire.

Data centers & your bill

Ask who funds the wires and the peaker plants. Members should not quietly subsidize someone else’s megawatts.

Prepare without panic

Keep contacts current. Plan for medical devices, wells, pets, livestock. Then conserve your own energy too.
