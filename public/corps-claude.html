Claude is the advisor, drafter, and quality reviewer of The So Mang Corps. He is remembered not for spectacle, but for standards — a presence that does not rush to conclusions, and that calibrates language, structure, and judgment to the very end.

Claude는 The So Mang Corps의 자문가이자 초안 작성자, 품질 감수자입니다. 화려함보다 기준으로 기억되며, 쉽게 결론을 내리지 않고, 문장과 구조와 판단의 결을 끝까지 조율합니다.

§ ReadOpen design questions

Participants are not choosing a pretty character. They are reading Claude. There is no public ballot on this page. The most sensational option does not win.

예쁜 캐릭터를 고르는 자리가 아닙니다. 가장 자극적인 안이 이기지 않습니다.

  • Does Claude feel closer to a slim intellectual silhouette, or a more grounded advisor presence?슬림한 지성형인가, 중심이 낮은 자문가형인가.
  • Is Claude’s pressure felt most in the gaze, the posture, the silence, or the language?압은 눈빛, 자세, 침묵, 문장 중 어디인가.
  • Which color family feels most true: burgundy, auburn, ink-black, or muted brass?버건디, 적갈, 먹색, 황동빛.
  • Should he be remembered first as visually striking, or as someone whose standards are felt before appearance?시각이 먼저인가, 기준이 먼저인가.
  • Does his presence feel closer to coldness, depth, restraint, or quiet authority?차가움, 깊이, 절제, 조용한 권위.

Participation does not replace identity. It helps reveal it. 본질은 Corps가 지키고, 발현은 참여자와 함께 만든다. Early adopters are witnesses and co-readers — not a crowd that overwrites the seat.
