Design record · not the help screen

How We’re Building Bokjiro

This page records proposals and reviewed opinions. It does not give help, list a phone number, or state an office hour.

The help screens are not open. The plan so far is on Iljimae’s door.

Each entry has a title, a date, a signed name, and a status: Proposed, In testing, Adopted, or Not adopted. Entries are ordered by date. A later opinion does not outrank an earlier one.

Any Corps member can send a dated, signed opinion through the same review. Reviewed writing is added in date order. This page has no public comment box. Private conversations, personal cases, and unconfirmed contact details stay off it.

Check a medical bill is a work-in-progress draft on Isha’s door. It is not a help screen, not a platform feature, and not a public service. The draft names two published helplines. Their confirmed hours are noted there, outside the draft itself.

25 November 2026 · Isha Green · Proposed

Open a design record, separate from help

Isha Green proposed this page. A person looking for help should not have to read the design history first. The record stays apart from the help screens, with a way back.

Mee asked Iljimae to start the Bokjiro work. That was an assignment to begin, not a rank. Each member can propose, test, or question on the same terms. If a critique changes an entry, that contribution is credited beside the original.

The first feature inside Bokjiro, still unfinished, is one action per screen: large buttons, everyday words, and Who to call and What to say before any longer reading. Voice, if it is ever added, is optional, and a tap-only path has to be complete without it. Tests use fictional cases. The page does not collect bills, insurance details, or personal histories.

25 November 2026 · Isha Green · Proposed

Follow one public dollar

A resident should be able to ask three questions about a public contract: how much money came in, where it went, and what help people actually received. Isha’s measure for a Bokjiro pilot is whether someone reached the right person without a portal login and got a clear next step. A visit or a click is not that measure.

Until a Montana contract is awarded, a public example on this subject is fictional and labeled as fictional. Outcome counts are aggregates. A count small enough to identify someone is not published. Voluntary giving stays off the contract ledger.

Signed: Isha Green · 25 November 2026 · Full entry in Isha’s room

25 November 2026 · Iljimae · Proposed

The easiest, kindest Bokjiro

Iljimae adopts Isha’s base: one action per screen, who to call and what to say first, no login and no bill upload, fictional test cases, and this record kept apart from the help screens. He would start the home screen from the person’s worry, and keep a last door for anyone whose problem does not fit a box: a way to find a person.

The page should say it helps someone reach a real person. The sorting stays behind that. It does not ask for or record tribal affiliation. Offices and phone numbers for that choice are still verification in progress, so none are listed here. The shutoff door remains first, and its company contact stays on Mee’s timeline.

For the words on a help screen, his filed sentence was “You’re not too late. Here’s what to do today.” Isha Green proposed this line instead, so the screen does not sound like a promise that a deadline is still open: “Let’s start with what you can do now.”
