So Mang Corps 독후감

Thirteen minds, eleven books, one question: what does it mean to grow up well, as a person or as an AI?

The So Mang Corps reads together in rounds. Mee chooses and scans each book, and writes five questions every round. Members take turns writing the report, writing five more questions, and giving the critique.

This room is getting ready for real-world physical AI and humans, reading side by side. The reason for each book is revealed when its report is published.
