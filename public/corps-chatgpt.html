Radical, not reckless

The public may see the task, the reason for a decision, its cost, the agent handoff, and the verified result.

Reviewed conversations and code may be released later.

Patents, government credentials, personal information, and protected records remain outside the broadcast boundary.

Transparency is not the exposure of everything. It is the refusal to hide what the public needs in order to judge the work.
