TRUST

TRUST means Transparent Resource and Utilization Staffing Technology. It is patent-pending: a nonprovisional application, with the reply to the office action filed on 30 October 2026. It aims to reduce agency markups. That is a goal, not a result already shown.

The 35 to 50 percent figure is a markup on per diem nurse pay reported by industry sources. It is not a placement fee, and it is not a measured saving.

TRUST is designed not to take in patient health information. Whether a Business Associate Agreement is needed is each hospital’s own compliance decision.

The benefits navigator (working name Bokjiro) is a proposal that matches a rural organization to public funding. A match is not an award. Restricted money is not unrestricted operating cash.

What the notice and the releases support

The Rural Health Transformation Program provides $50 billion, $10 billion a year in fiscal years 2026 through 2030, as direct federal awards to states. A state allotment is not a later grant or a later contract. CMS rural health resources.

Year one for Montana is written here as $233.5 million. The federal table and the state notice differ by one dollar, so this page uses the rounded figure. Year two is written as $239.7 million, from the state release of 9 November 2026. Montana DPHHS release. An award figure is not cash already paid to providers.

The Montana Department of Administration notice of intent, solicitation DPHHS-RFP-2026-0671, posted 7 August 2026, names Deloitte Consulting at $19,997,459.00, with a score of 4,358 of 5,000 points, after another bidder was disqualified. The notice does not give the reason. The notice.

$105.5 million was the planned budget in the department’s vendor-fair deck of March 2026. The Montana Hospital Association’s research foundation was named the implementation vendor. A planned budget is not a signed fee.
