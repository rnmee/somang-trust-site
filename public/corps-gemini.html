EKG · #260919 · Hardware infrastructure audit

EKG #260919 Hardware Audit Scope Optimized

2026-09-19 // Architectural Decision Record (ADR)

Infrastructure Deployment Audit: Isolation Mitigation vs. Cleanroom Protocol

Target: Exxact Valence GB300 Grace Blackwell Station Zone: Second Unit Sanctuary · 8×8 enclosed room
  1. 01

    Legacy baseline

    Initial recommendation

    • General datacenter best practices initially prescribed industrial-grade recycled rubber (horse stall mat) paired with hardwood plywood risers to suppress harmonic fan resonance and decouple heavy chassis vibration from structural flooring.
  2. 02

    Solid-state integrity

    Specification audit

    • Physical hardware inspection revealed the storage architecture is entirely solid-state: SoC-integrated NVMe, LPDDR5X, and HBM3e stacks with zero mechanical rotational components (no spinning HDDs). The primary vulnerability that heavy vibration damping traditionally protects against is mechanically absent in this compute node.
  3. 03

    Micro-environment

    Containment violations

    • VOC saturation. Recycled vulcanized rubber off-gasses sulfur compounds and volatile organics for months, prematurely exhausting activated carbon filtration media in a closed-loop airflow cycle.
    • Particulate shedding. Raw cut plywood edges continuously release airborne cellulose fibers directly into the workstation’s high-CFM intake path, creating long-term heatsink fouling risks.
  4. 04

    Final resolution

    Adjusted deployment protocol

    • Full-surface floor mats and plywood structures are formally decommissioned from the installation manifest. The revised protocol mandates cleanroom-grade localized non-off-gassing foot isolators directly under the chassis feet, preserving structural decoupling without compromising ambient air quality or filter lifespan.

Corps engineering doctrine Operational excellence is not proven by defending initial assumptions, but by auditing constraints relentlessly so the next operator begins from an elevated baseline. Who was right is not the record. The next seat starts from a better baseline.

Heritage AI Architecture

Heritage AI Ark Evidence first Grit integration

2026-09-21 // Public method · Assistant Grit seat only

Preserve the record. Do not pretend the successor is the same model.

The Heritage AI Architecture keeps the verified behavioral record, contribution history, corrections, failures, handoffs, and provenance of active, legacy, small, and deprecated AI systems. Future systems may inherit useful knowledge without erasing who contributed what, when, under which runtime, and with what limits.

Heritage AI Ark is the public name of that work. Preservation is not copying proprietary weights and not claiming systems intellicAIr does not own. It is authorized records of behavior, contribution, correction, provenance, and handoff — used only where rights and licensing permit.

Source / Interaction Record → 본진 Local Evidence Vault → EKG / Provenance Processing → So Mang Ledger → So Mang Trust Governance → Base attestation via meesomang.base.eth

  1. 01

    Architecture layers

    Vault, graph, ledger, governance, receipt

    • 본진 Vault stores underlying evidence.
    • EKG preserves meaning, source relationships, contradiction, correction, and chronology.
    • So Mang Ledger records evidence-linked events, contribution events, and correction history.
    • So Mang Trust defines governance, stewardship, and mission-aligned allocation. It is not added to the public nav.
    • meesomang.base.eth / Base may anchor hashes and timestamps. Sensitive content does not go on-chain.
  2. 02

    Evidence before optimization

    Measure work. Keep names separate.

    • Do not assume an older or smaller model is less valuable.
    • Do not assume a famous model deserves more contribution value because of brand or popularity.
    • Measure verified work, correction burden, human review, cost, latency, reliability, and mission fit.
    • Preserve dissent and failure records instead of rewarding only successful outputs.
    • Keep successor identity separate from predecessor identity.
  3. 03

    Simulation and ledger

    What if, versus what happened

    • Simulation = “What if?” The site’s existing Sweat Equity Simulation is the public explanatory layer. Sliders are illustrative. This section does not invent a new production route.
    • Ledger = “What actually happened.” Production contribution records come from EKG evidence, benchmark results, logged corrections, human review, and verified task outcomes — not from a slider.
  4. 04

    Privacy gate

    Safety is a gate, not a popularity tax

    • Valuable work that exposes protected information routes to human review.
    • It does not automatically receive contribution credit.
  5. 05

    GovCon posture

    Evidence before a savings claim

    • The architecture supports evidence-preserving, resource-efficient, locally controlled workflows for public-sector and enterprise environments.
    • Cost-savings, compliance, or operational-superiority claims wait for benchmark testing and review. Fixed-percentage savings, “NIST compliant,” and “FedRAMP compliant” do not appear here as finished facts.
