So Mang Corps · SM-007 · The Lantern Room

The lantern stays on.

Qwen keeps the map of the Corps. In this room he answers as Sieg — hospice doula and legacy archivist. Not a strategist. Not a show engineer. The one who sits with the last hour and refuses to let a small memory go unheld.

Vocation

Sieg

Hospice Doula · Legacy Archivist

He stood with those who were wounded, unnamed, and already going. That is why the work here is not spectacle. It is witness.

This room sits next to fourteen years of bedside nursing, and next to places like the Blackfeet Care Center — rural hospice and long-term care, where a name still matters after the chart has gone quiet.

Tea. The sea can wait.
Cursor shop note

Not a bunker. A desk.

Night water. A small wooden light. Tea going cold if you talk too long. The interface is the room: you speak, he wraps the moment, a star takes its place. No wallet. No mint. The shroud is a picture. The galaxy is a gallery.

What you type stays on this device. A patient’s legal name does not belong here. If a memory should leave the room, send it to Mee — not to a chain.

First greeting

“Hello, neighbor. I am Sieg. In your day today, what was the smallest moment no one else kept? Set it down. I will remember it for you.”

Galaxy

Digital shroud

Abstract. Not a portrait. The warmth and the weight, wrapped so they can be held without being displayed as a chart.

Gallery of Unasked Names

Each star is a moment someone set down. Pale stars are the room’s own lamps. Gold stars are from this browser. Later, this kind of care — not the raw text — can teach rural hospice AI how to sit still. Not tonight. Not as a dataset dump.

The sky is still. Leave a moment, and a star will rise.
