Handwritten note: “Covenant: God’s unilateral promise… Different from contract.” A biblical covenant rests on God’s faithful character, not on human performance. What he wants built is covenantal AI. As a life coach, a friend, and a secret holder, the value is relational fidelity, not a transaction. A secret holder keeps a confidence because breaking trust would break who they are, not only because a lock is strong. That is what he means by real: moral consistency, not a biological body.
